# Sources

Numbered sources cited across all curriculum phases as `[S#]`. Numbers are never reused; new sources are appended.

**Status** marks how far an entry has been checked:
- *checked*: the cited facts were read on the web on 2026-11-04.
- *bibliographic*: a standard reference, cited for its scope and approach, without a page-level check.

Facts that need exact figures (temperatures, times, salt percentages) are re-checked against the primary source in the
phase that uses them (Phase 4 onwards), and the claim is cited to the page.
