# KM — Kitchen management

Planning and timing, culinary math, purchasing and cost, waste, and equipment. Entries follow the format of the
[competency framework §1.2](../04-competency-framework.md#12-the-entry-format). The mapping block under each entry is
generated from the outline.

### KM-PLAN — Planning and timing

**Elements:** a backwards timeline from service; parallel tasks; control points; make-ahead and holding; multi-day
production; adapting the plan when something slips.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: working back from the serving time; long tasks first.
- L3: parallel tasks and their dependencies; holding times.
- L4: multi-day plans; buffers.
- L5: planning for guests and constraints.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: writes and follows a timeline for a meal.
- [J] L4: plans a menu over several days with control points.
- [M] L5: every course on time for guests, absorbing slips, without a recipe.

**Independence:**
- S1 (2): with a template.
- S2 (3): writes own.
- S3–S4 (4): multi-day.
- S5 (5): masters.

**Assessment:** written timelines compared with the timestamped log; re-tested at `MS-S2`, `MS-S3`, `MS-S4`
and the capstone.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A backwards timeline from a template for a two-component meal; both components out within 10 minutes of the plan | written, log |
| 3 | Own timeline for a three-component plate with parallel tasks; every component out within 5 minutes of the plan | written, log |
| 4 | A multi-day plan with control points for a menu; the log shows every control point met or a reasoned change | written, log |
| 5 | Five productions over at least 8 weeks (including guests and the capstone): every course within 5 minutes of the plan, slips recorded and absorbed | log, written |

**Not yet:** no written plan; everything started at once; the last component cold or late; a plan that ignores resting
and holding.

### KM-MATH — Culinary math

**Elements:** units and conversions; scaling recipes; yield percentage; baker's percentages; ratios; cost per portion.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: metric conversions and simple scaling.
- L3: yield % (as-purchased versus edible portion); baker's percentages.
- L4: costing a menu; scaling with non-linear ingredients (salt, spices, leaveners, cooking times).

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: scales and converts formulas correctly.
- [J] L4: costs a menu from yields and prices, and adjusts portions to a budget.

**Independence:**
- S1 (2): with a calculator sheet.
- S2 (3): alone.
- S3–S5 (4): costs and scales menus.

**Assessment:** calculation quiz items; costing sheets checked against receipts; re-tested at `MS-S3`, `MS-S4` and the
capstone.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Conversions and scaling items at 80 % | quiz |
| 3 | Yield and baker's-percentage items at 80 %; a recipe scaled and a bread formula written in baker's percentages, both cooked successfully | quiz, measure, written |
| 4 | A menu costed per portion from measured yields; within 10 % of the receipts; non-linear scaling handled with a reason | measure, written |

**Not yet:** doubling salt and leavener blindly; costing from the price of the whole item without yield.

### KM-PURC — Purchasing and budgeting

**Elements:** shopping lists by shop and section; quantities for the number of guests; where to buy what; budgets; cost
per portion.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: list structure and quantities per person.
- L4: shop types and their strengths; buying for a week; buying for a budget.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L2: writes a complete shopping list.
- [J] L4: buys for a week or a dinner within a budget, with little waste and good quality.

**Independence:**
- S1–S2 (1–2): with templates.
- S3–S5 (4): plans and buys alone.

**Assessment:** lists and receipts; written budgets; re-tested at `MS-S3`, `MS-S4` and the capstone.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A complete shopping list for a meal from a recipe; nothing forgotten | written, checklist |
| 3 | Lists and quantities for three meals for the household; actual purchases within 15 % of the planned quantities | written, measure |
| 4 | A week or a guest dinner bought within budget: quantities within 10 % of what was used, quality judged, and the receipts against the plan | measure, written |

**Not yet:** a second trip to the shop; buying double; no idea of the total cost.

### KM-WAST — Using ingredients fully

**Elements:** using trim (stocks, sauces, pickles); leftovers planned into the next meal; batch cooking; storage that
prevents waste; measuring waste.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: what trim is good for.
- L3: planning leftovers; batch cooking.
- L4: menus designed around whole ingredients.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: turns trim and leftovers into food.
- [J] L4: plans a week around whole ingredients and measures the waste.

**Independence:**
- S1 (2): guided.
- S2 (3): by habit.
- S3–S5 (4): plans for it.

**Assessment:** a waste log (weights); photos; re-tested in the `MS-S3` week plan.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Uses vegetable trim in a stock and logs what was thrown away in one week | log, photo |
| 3 | Three meals using planned leftovers or trim; the waste log shows less discarded than the first week | log, photo |
| 4 | A week plan around a whole bird or fish and seasonal produce, with every part used; edible waste under 5 % of the food bought by weight, logged | log, measure, written |

**Not yet:** trim in the bin by habit; leftovers forgotten in the fridge; no idea how much is thrown away.

### KM-EQPT — Equipment

**Elements:** choosing tools and appliances; using each to its strength; care and maintenance; calibration
(thermometers, scales, the oven); knife sharpening (shared with `TS-KNF`); Good and Best choices (Phase 1 §9.4).

**Knowledge:**
- L2: the basic equipment and its uses.
- L3: materials and their care; calibration.
- L4: choosing what to buy next for the learner's goals; adapting techniques to the equipment at hand.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: maintains and calibrates equipment.
- [J] L4: chooses equipment for a production and works around what is missing.

**Independence:**
- S1 (2): guided.
- S2–S3 (3): maintains.
- S4–S5 (4): chooses and adapts.

**Assessment:** calibration logs; maintenance photos; written equipment plans; re-tested in the `MS-S4` prep days.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Equipment items at 80 %; the right pan and tool chosen for three recipes | quiz, photo |
| 3 | A calibration log for thermometer, scale and oven; cast iron seasoned and carbon steel kept rust-free (photographed) | log, measure, photo |
| 4 | An equipment plan for a guest dinner (what goes in which pan and oven, and when), followed in the log; one missing tool worked around, with the reason | written, log |

**Not yet:** an uncalibrated thermometer; a rusty pan; buying gadgets before the basics; no plan for oven space.
