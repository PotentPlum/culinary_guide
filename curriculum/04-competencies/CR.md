# CR — Creativity and adaptability

Composition, menus, originality and improvisation. Entries follow the format of the
[competency framework §1.2](../04-competency-framework.md#12-the-entry-format). The mapping block under each entry is
generated from the outline.

Creativity is judged on **intent and coherence**: is there a clear idea, does every element serve it, and do the guests
experience it? It is not judged on novelty for its own sake.

### CR-COMP — Composing a dish

**Elements:** main element; sauce; garnish; contrast (texture, temperature, colour, taste); portion and proportion; the
plate as a whole.

**Knowledge:**
- L1–2: the parts of a composed dish.
- L3: the logic of balance and contrast.
- L4: composing for a menu and a tradition.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [J] L3: composes a balanced plate from a brief.
- [J] L4: composes dishes with a clear idea, for a menu and for guests.

**Independence:**
- S2 (1): awareness.
- S3 (3): from briefs.
- S4–S5 (4): designs.

**Assessment:** written dish plans (a component table and a sketch); photos; tasters; re-tested at `MS-S3` and
`MS-S4`.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Names the parts of three composed dishes and what each contributes; plates one from a framework | written, photo |
| 3 | Two plates composed from briefs, with a component table (main, sauce, garnish, contrast) and the tasters' judgment of balance | written, photo, tasters |
| 4 | Three dishes composed for a menu with a written idea; the photos and guests' cards show every element serving it | written, photo, tasters |

**Not yet:** too many elements; garnish for colour only; no contrast; a sauce that fights the main element.

### CR-MENU — Designing menus

**Elements:** progression (light to rich, or a deliberate arc); balance of techniques, tastes, textures and colours
across courses; season; feasibility in a home kitchen (oven space, timing, make-ahead); drinks; guests' needs.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: menu structure and balance.
- L3–4: feasibility analysis; theme and season; adapting for guests.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [J] L3: designs a balanced, feasible menu for the household.
- [J] L4: designs a themed menu for guests, with drinks and a dietary need.

**Independence:**
- S3 (2): with a checklist.
- S4–S5 (4): designs alone.

**Assessment:** written menus and rationales; feasibility checked against the log; guests' cards; re-tested at
`MS-S3`, `MS-S4` and the capstone.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A three-course menu checked against the balance checklist (no repeated main technique, colour or dominant taste) | written, checklist |
| 3 | A week menu or a dinner menu with a rationale and a feasibility check (oven space, timing); cooked as planned | written, log |
| 4 | A themed three- or four-course menu for guests with drinks and a dietary need, a rationale, and the guests' cards confirming the progression and balance | written, tasters, log |

**Not yet:** three rich courses in a row; two dishes needing the oven at the same time and temperature; no thought for
the guest with a need.

### CR-IMPV — Improvising under constraints

**Elements:** pantry cooking; mystery baskets; limited time; missing equipment; unexpected diets; using what's in season
or left over.

**Knowledge:**
- L2–3: pantry building; frameworks that adapt (a soup, a braise, a salad, a frittata).
- L4: improvising complete meals from first principles.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [J] L3: cooks a good meal from what is at hand within a time limit.
- [J] L4: cooks a multi-course meal from a mystery basket for guests.

**Independence:**
- S2 (1): awareness.
- S3–S4 (3): pantry meals.
- S5 (4): mystery baskets for guests.

**Assessment:** a photo of the basket or pantry with the time limit; the result; tasters; re-tested in the `MS-S3`
week plan.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A pantry meal from a framework within 45 minutes, with the pantry photographed | photo, log |
| 3 | Three meals from what was at hand (photographed first) within 45 minutes, without a recipe; the tasters judge each good | photo, log, tasters |
| 4 | A two- or three-course meal from a mystery basket (put together by someone else) for guests within 3 hours, with a written plan made in the first 15 minutes; every course judged good | photo, log, tasters |

**Not yet:** a shopping trip whenever something is missing; one go-to dish for every situation; panic when the plan
changes.

### CR-ORIG — Original dishes and a personal point of view

**Elements:** an idea or point of view; development from idea to tested dish; drawing on traditions without copying;
a personal style across dishes.

**Knowledge:**
- L1–2: where ideas come from (an ingredient, a memory, a technique, a tradition).
- L3–4: the development process (idea, prototype, test, refine, write up).

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [J] L3: develops an original dish from an idea to a tested, written recipe.
- [J] L4: a small body of original dishes with a recognisable point of view.

**Independence:**
- S3 (1): awareness.
- S4 (3): develops dishes.
- S5 (4): has a point of view.

**Assessment:** development logs (idea, tests, changes); written recipes; guests' cards; re-tested in the capstone menu
and test cooks.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Describes three dish ideas and where each comes from; develops one with guidance | written |
| 3 | An original dish developed over at least two test rounds, logged, written as a recipe, and judged good by the tasters | log, written, tasters |
| 4 | At least three original dishes (one in the capstone menu) with a stated idea and a recognisable style; development logs; the guests' cards and the defence show the idea was understood | written, log, tasters |

**Not yet:** a known dish renamed; novelty without a reason; never tested before serving.
