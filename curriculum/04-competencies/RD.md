### RD-RATO — Ratios and formulas

**Elements:** the core ratios (vinaigrette, roux and sauce, custard, brine, pastry, batter, bread hydration, cure);
baker's percentages; building a dish from a ratio instead of a recipe.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: the core ratios and what each part does.
- L3: how changing one part changes the result.
- L4: deriving a formula for a new preparation from known ratios.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: cooks from ratios without a recipe.
- [J] L4: writes and adjusts formulas for a new purpose.

**Independence:**
- S2 (2): with a ratio card.
- S3 (3): from memory.
- S4–S5 (4): derives formulas.

**Assessment:** quiz; dishes cooked from ratios; written formulas; re-tested in the `MS-S2` sauce.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Core ratios at 80 %; a vinaigrette and a roux-thickened sauce made from a ratio card | quiz, photo |
| 3 | Three preparations cooked from ratios from memory, weighed and to standard; one ratio changed on purpose and the effect recorded | measure, photo, written |
| 4 | Two formulas derived for new preparations (for example a custard for a new flavour, a brine for a new cut), written in percentages, cooked and to standard | written, measure, photo |

**Not yet:** cooking only from recipes; ratios learned without knowing what each part does.

### RD-ADPT — Adaptation and substitution

**Elements:** substituting ingredients; changing quantities; changing equipment; adapting for diets and allergens;
adapting to season and availability; predicting the effect.

**Knowledge:**
- L1–2: common substitutions and their limits.
- L3: the function of each ingredient in a dish, as the basis for a substitution.
- L4–5: adapting complex dishes and menus for diets without a lesser result.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [J] L3: substitutes one ingredient and predicts the effect.
- [J] L4: adapts a dish for a diet, a quantity or equipment and gets a comparable result.
- [M] L5: adapts on the fly, consistently, and teaches the reasoning.

**Independence:**
- S1 (1): awareness.
- S2 (2): with a guide.
- S3 (3): alone.
- S4 (4): adapts dishes for guests.
- S5 (5): masters.

**Assessment:** written predictions before cooking, compared with the result; tasters; re-tested in the `MS-S4` menu.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A substitution from a guide (for example a different vinegar or herb) with the effect noted | written, form |
| 3 | Three substitutions reasoned from the ingredient's function, with the effect predicted in writing and confirmed | written, photo, form |
| 4 | Two dishes adapted (one for a diet or allergen, one for quantity or equipment), with predictions; the tasters judge the adapted dish as good as the original | written, tasters, photo |
| 5 | Five adaptations over at least 8 weeks under three conditions (a diet, equipment, no recipe and on the day), each predicted and confirmed; a short guide to adapting one dish family | written, tasters, photo |

**Not yet:** substituting by name ("cream is cream"); a gluten-free version that is clearly worse; no prediction before
cooking.

### RD-TEST — Systematic testing

**Elements:** a question; one variable at a time; controls; side-by-side tasting; blind comparison; recording; drawing
conclusions.

**Knowledge:**
- L2: why only one variable changes at a time.
- L3: setting up a controlled comparison; blind tasting.
- L4: designing a test series for recipe development; enough repetitions to trust the result.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: runs a controlled comparison and records it.
- [J] L4: designs a test series to develop or fix a recipe, and acts on the result.

**Independence:**
- S1–S2 (1–2): guided comparisons.
- S3 (3): runs own.
- S4–S5 (4): designs series.

**Assessment:** test logs; blind comparison results from tasters; re-tested in the capstone test cooks.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A guided comparison with one variable changed, recorded on a template | log, form |
| 3 | Two comparisons of their own design (question, variable, control, blind tasting), logged, with a conclusion the data support | log, written, tasters |
| 4 | A test series that develops or fixes a recipe over at least three rounds, with the changes and results logged and the final version better by blind tasting | log, written, tasters |

**Not yet:** changing three things at once; no record; conclusions from one tasting; the tester knowing which sample is
which.

### RD-WRIT — Writing recipes and technique notes

**Elements:** a recipe's structure (yield, ingredients in order of use with weights, method, cues, timings, holding);
technique notes; the journal; consistent terms (`CK-TERM`).

**Knowledge:**
- L2: what a reproducible recipe contains.
- L3: writing cues, not only times; noting variables.
- L4: writing for another cook; testing a recipe by having someone else follow it.

**Practical ability and judgment:**
- [S] L3: writes recipes from their own cooking that work when repeated.
- [J] L4: writes recipes another person can follow to the same result.

**Independence:**
- S1–S2 (2): with a template.
- S3 (3): alone.
- S4–S5 (4): for others.

**Assessment:** written recipes against a checklist; a recipe cooked by another person from the learner's text;
re-tested in the `MS-S4` plan.

**Success criteria:**

| Level | A successful demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A recipe written on the template from a dish they cooked, complete by the checklist | written, checklist |
| 3 | Three recipes with weights, cues and timings, each repeated by the learner a week later to the same result | written, photo |
| 4 | Two recipes cooked by another person from the text alone; the results photographed and matching the learner's version | written, photo, tasters |

**Not yet:** "a handful", "cook until done"; ingredients missing from the method; recipes only the writer can follow.
