Every knowledge-check item marked as a safety item in the course files of Phase 5 is listed here, word for word, with
the row of [assessment §9](04-assessment.md#9-safety-figures-used-in-assessment) it rests on. The S3 review made this
read a condition for Phase 6 ([Phase 5 §9.3](05-course-development.md#93-gaps-and-open-questions-review-answers-2026-11-04)):
**a person other than the author reads every safety item and logs the result.** The author drafted the items and the
mapping, so the author can't be the reader. The program's owner can be.

Phase 6 runs in parallel (S5 review answer 4). The lesson files it writes stay drafts until this log is complete and
every disagreement is resolved (Phase 6 §3, in `06-lesson-development.md`).

## How to read

For each row:
1. **Read the question and the answer.** Would a careful home cook, reading only the lesson, choose that answer?
2. **Compare it with the §9 row** named in the "§9 row" column. Several rows are separated by "; ". Where an item rests
   on a course rule rather than a figure (allergens, burns and fires, knives, tier 3 equipment, hygiene steps), the
   column names the lesson that teaches it ("— `SAF-1.M4.L1`"). Read that lesson's outline in its course file.
3. **Write a verdict** in the Verdict column:
   - `agrees`: the answer matches §9 (or the lesson), the wrong options are clearly wrong, and the wording is clear;
   - `disagrees`: anything else. Write why in the Note column: the answer is wrong, a wrong option is also right,
     the §9 row is the wrong one, the question is unclear, or the figure differs from the source.
4. **Leave the other columns as they are.** The validator checks that the question, answer and why still match the
   course file word for word, so a later edit to an item shows up here.

What the reader does **not** need to check: the program's structure, the number of items, the pass rules or the
links. The validator checks those.

**When an item is changed** after a disagreement, the author copies the new text into its row, clears the verdict and
records the fix in the log below; the reader then reads the row again.
