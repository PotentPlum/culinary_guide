# S1 — Foundations (*Fundament*), 170 h

Phase 2 outline for stage S1. The conventions (row format, tags, support levels) are in
[`../02-curriculum-outline.md`](../02-curriculum-outline.md#conventions). The purpose and exit gate are in
[Phase 1 §6.2](../01-vision-architecture.md#62-stage-profiles).

**Stage question:** can I work safely, organised and with control in my kitchen, and taste what I make?
**Entry:** none. `ORI-1`'s diagnostic can waive lessons for experienced learners, but never `SAF-1`.

Course order follows the dependencies:
1. `ORI-1`, then `SAF-1` and `WRK-1`, then `EQP-1`.
2. Then `KNF-1` and `TST-1`, which can run in parallel. `TST-1`'s tasting work is reused in every later course.
3. Then `HEA-1`.
4. Then `EGG-1`, `VEG-1` and `STA-1`.

### SAF-1 — Food safety and hygiene I (15.5 h)

**Purpose:** prevent foodborne illness and kitchen accidents at home. This is the non-negotiable basis of every
practical. Follows the Dutch Voedingscentrum guidance [S21][S22], with the FDA *Food Code* where a measurable figure is
needed [S25] (Phase 4 review).
**Prerequisites:** ORI-1
**Equipment:** an instant-read thermometer, a fridge thermometer, separate boards.
**Outcomes:**
- [K] Explains how food makes people ill and which foods and people are high-risk. `SC-MICR→1`
- [S] Works hygienically and prevents cross-contamination by habit. `SA-HYG→3`
- [S] Controls temperature for storing, cooking, cooling and reheating, with a calibrated thermometer. `SA-TEMP→3`
- [K] Recognises the 14 EU allergens and prevents cross-contact. `SA-ALLG→2`
- [S] Prevents and handles burns, cuts and fires. `SA-PHYS→2`

**Competencies on completion:** SA-HYG 3 · SA-TEMP 3 · SA-ALLG 2 · SA-PHYS 2 · SC-MICR 1 · IN-STO 3
