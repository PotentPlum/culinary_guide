# S4 — Advanced (*Gevorderd*), 210 h

Phase 2 outline for stage S4. The conventions are in [`../02-curriculum-outline.md`](../02-curriculum-outline.md#conventions);
the purpose and exit gate are in [Phase 1 §6.2](../01-vision-architecture.md#62-stage-profiles).

**Stage question:** can I execute advanced and classical techniques, compose plates and menus, and develop recipes?
**Entry:** `MS-S3` passed.

Course order:
- `CLS-4`, `CUI-4`, `PRO-4`, `PST-4`, `MOD-4`, `PLT-4` and `RDV-4` can run in any order.
- `MNU-4` comes last, because it brings plating, recipe development and kitchen management together.

Most exercises are briefs (Phase 1 §6.6).

---

### CLS-4 — Classical French repertoire (30 h)

**Purpose:** the classical sauce system and its derivatives; a domestic demi-glace; consommé; classical cuts, garnishes
and preparations; naming; how classical cuisine became modern cuisine [S11][S12].
**Prerequisites:** MS-S3
**Equipment:** stockpot, fine sieve or chinois, cheesecloth, turning knife (optional).
**Outcomes:**
- [K] Uses the classical system: mother sauces, derivatives, garnishes and names. `CK-CLAS→4`, `CK-TERM→4`
- [S] Makes classical sauces and their derivatives to a refined standard. `TS-SAU→5`
- [S] Clarifies a consommé. `TS-STK→4`
- [S] Cuts and cooks classical vegetable garnishes precisely. `TS-KNF→5`, `TS-VEG→5`

**Competencies on completion:** CK-CLAS 4 · CK-TERM 4 · TS-SAU 5 · TS-STK 4 · TS-KNF 5 · TS-VEG 5 · TS-PRO 4 · SC-PROT 4 · FL-SEAS 5 · FL-ARCH 4

techniques, and a regional menu. Each deep dive follows the same 10-hour template; Phase 5 writes the content for each
