# S3 — Integration (*Integratie*), 270 h

Phase 2 outline for stage S3. The conventions are in [`../02-curriculum-outline.md`](../02-curriculum-outline.md#conventions);
the purpose and exit gate are in [Phase 1 §6.2](../01-vision-architecture.md#62-stage-profiles).

**Stage question:** can I combine techniques into complete dishes, control flavour on purpose, and plan and manage my
kitchen?
**Entry:** `MS-S2` passed.

Course order:
1. `SAF-3` comes first. Phase 1 §7.3 listed it after `SLW-3`, but `SLW-3`, `GRD-3` and `PRS-3` need it (change log).
2. Then `FLV-3`, which `VEG-3` and `CUI-3` build on.
3. Then the other courses, which can run in any order that respects their prerequisites.

From this stage on, most exercises are frameworks or briefs (Phase 1 §6.6).

### FIR-3 — Barbecue and live fire (28.5 h)

**Purpose:** cooking over a charcoal or wood fire: building and controlling the fire; direct grilling of meat, fish,
vegetables and bread; indirect roasting with the lid on; low-and-slow barbecue (collagen, bark, the stall); cooking in
the embers; a live-fire meal for guests. Added in Phase 3 (R2). Wood smoke is taught as a flavour of barbecue;
smoking as a preservation method (cold smoking, smoked cures) stays outside the core (Phase 3 §2).
**Prerequisites:** MS-S2, SAF-3, SLW-3
**Equipment:** tier 2 (the grill items): a lidded charcoal grill (Good: a 57 cm kettle; Best: a kamado or a kettle with a hinged grate),
a chimney starter, heat-resistant gloves, long tongs, a probe thermometer (Best: a two-probe wireless one for fire and
meat). Without outdoor space: a cast-iron grill pan and the oven grill teach the browning, but not fire control; the
learner then reaches `TS-FIRE` 2 at most, and the record says so.
**Outcomes:**
- [K] Explains how a charcoal fire gives off heat (radiant and convective), and how zones, vents and the lid control it. `TS-FIRE→2`
- [S] Grills meat, fish, vegetables and flatbread over direct heat, and roasts by indirect heat, to a target doneness. `TS-FIRE→3`, `TS-PRO→4`
- [S] Cooks a low-and-slow barbecue (pork shoulder or ribs) to a target texture, with a temperature log. `TS-CMB→4`, `SA-HRPR→4`
- [J] Plans a live-fire meal: the fire plan, the order of cooking, and safety around fire and smoke. `KM-PLAN→4`, `SA-PHYS→4`

**Competencies on completion:** TS-FIRE 3 · TS-PRO 4 · TS-CMB 4 · TS-DRY 4 · TS-VEG 4 · TS-SAU 4 · SC-PROT 4 · SA-HRPR 4 · SA-PHYS 4 · FL-ARCH 3 · KM-PLAN 4 · JD-CUES 4 · IN-FSH 3
