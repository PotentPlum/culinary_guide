# S5 — Mastery and independence (*Meesterschap*), 110 h

Phase 2 outline for stage S5. The conventions are in [`../02-curriculum-outline.md`](../02-curriculum-outline.md#conventions);
the purpose and exit gate are in [Phase 1 §6.2](../01-vision-architecture.md#62-stage-profiles) and the capstone
outline is in [Phase 1 §10.5](../01-vision-architecture.md#105-capstone-outline).

**Stage question:** can I cook anything well without a recipe, create my own food, and prove it under pressure?
**Entry:** `MS-S4` passed.

Course order:
1. `REV-5` can start first, so its gap analysis steers the rest.
2. `IND-5` and `RDV-5` run in parallel.
3. `CAP-5` comes last.

Almost every exercise is a brief (Phase 1 §6.6). The lessons here are mostly *structured practice*: the teaching is
done, and what remains is consistent, independent performance.

---

### IND-5 — Cooking without recipes (43.5 h)

**Purpose:** a series of briefs: mystery baskets, cooking from the pantry, recreating dishes from a written or tasted
description, constraint challenges (time, equipment, diet), and a troubleshooting clinic.
**Prerequisites:** MS-S4
**Equipment:** the full home kitchen.
**Outcomes:**
- [J] Chooses an approach for any unfamiliar ingredient or dish from first principles. `JD-CHSE→5`, `CK-METH→5`
- [S] Improvises good food under constraints. `CR-IMPV→4`, `RD-ADPT→5`
- [J] Diagnoses and rescues dishes under pressure, using the senses first. `JD-TRBL→5`, `FL-CORR→5`, `JD-CUES→5`
- [S] Plans and executes quickly and calmly. `KM-PLAN→5`

**Competencies on completion:** JD-CHSE 5 · CK-METH 5 · CR-IMPV 4 · RD-ADPT 5 · JD-TRBL 5 · FL-CORR 5 · JD-CUES 5 · FL-SENS 5 · KM-PLAN 5 · TS-DRY 5 · IN-VEG 5 · CK-CLAS 4

**Prerequisites:** MS-S4

### CAP-5 — Capstone (51 h)

**Prerequisites:** IND-5, RDV-5, REV-5
**Format:** see [Phase 1 §10.5](../01-vision-architecture.md#105-capstone-outline): a four-course dinner for 6–8
guests from a seasonal brief, with a budget, a dietary requirement and a featured ingredient. The menu must include:
- a stock- or emulsion-based sauce;
- a protein cooked to an exact doneness;
- a bread or pastry element made from scratch;
- a preserved element made at least a week ahead;
- a vegetable-led course.

There is no recipe, and dishes copied from course exercises don't count towards these requirements. The weighted rubric
comes in Phase 4.
